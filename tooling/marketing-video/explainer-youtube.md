How Macro works: email, chat, docs, tasks, calls, CRM and AI agents as one system

## Description

Your team's work is spread across dozens of tools, so neither your people nor your AI agents can follow it. Macro
puts email, chat, docs, tasks, calls, a CRM and AI agents into a single system with one shared memory, for less
than the tools it replaces. This 80-second walkthrough follows one demo call through Macro.

- Notion, Slack, Linear and Superhuman on their business plans cost $84 a seat per month, billed annually (list
  prices checked September 2026). Macro is $40 a seat, with every module included.
- Calls are recorded, transcribed and summarized into your team's memory.
- Agents work from that memory. They update the CRM, create tasks, propose meetings and draft replies, and
  nothing goes out until you confirm it.
- Tasks, email and messages land in one inbox. Mention a doc in a channel and everyone in it can open it.
- Search covers email, docs, tasks, messages and call transcripts in under 50 ms.
- Connect Claude Code, Codex or Cursor through Macro's MCP server, included on every plan.
